Connection guide

Use your gateway’s HTTPS hostname and a trusted certificate. Send Authorization: Bearer YOUR_TOKEN in a header; never put tokens in URLs. Limit: 120 requests per minute per token.

GET /api/v1/clients?period=24h&page=1&page_size=50

GET /api/v1/clients/10.20.0.10/traffic?period=24h

Periods: 1h, 24h, 7d, 30d, all. Pagination: 25–100 rows. API tokens stop working when revoked, expired, or when their administrator owner is disabled/downgraded.

Only Squid traffic is visible. DIRECT traffic, endpoint processes and encrypted page contents are not captured. Names are current DNS/IP labels; NAT and changing DHCP leases can make PC identity ambiguous.
